## Mid-Fi Testing
Note that video recordings of the Mid-Fi testing was collected but has not been posted on the public Github due to privacy reasons. The videos can be seen through contacting a member of Just 5 Vibes,


### Main findings of group 1:
- The "mystery" of the tracks was fun. The group discussed whose hand should go where and tested each row one by one to figure out what instrument was what. Then at the end they started to play their own music!

- The main problem was they got bored after the experience/mystery was solved. The dome interaction was simple due to the looping nature of the song and the limited time to experiment.

- They wanted more interactivity and more songs to play from. We could look in how proximity of hands alters sounds (maybe there's a special effect, see low pass filters, that can play)
- This would be similar to the reactable. 

### Main findings of group 2:

- Group 2 found it hard to link the different rows to different instruments. (From top to bottom of dome, we had vocals, harmony, drums, bass). Participants did not identify that instruments were separated by rows. They also did not talk as much as they were confused on which instrument does what. 
- They also did not seem to experiment as much as the avoided the top and bottom rows. The lack of visual feedback limited their ability to see that their actions have impact.

- However, they enjoy the mystery aspect of the dome.
- It was also suggested to change the angle of the dome so that is felt nicer to position the hands at the bottom of the dome (though that might not be necessary)
